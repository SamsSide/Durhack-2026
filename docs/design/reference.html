Dispatches
One component, three bands. A dispatch drops in at the top right under the top bar (keyframes "herald-in", 600 ms), stays about 6 s, and leaves upward. Only one shows at a time; a newer one replaces it. Triggers, from recentEvents: a war declared, a link restored (the count of merged events and settled disputes comes from the same batch), a nation deleted or fallen. Everything else stays in the ticker.
SettledAwarded to the Sunspire Dominion
The restore, on the map
While a nation is cut its border is a dotted cyan ring and its land is dimmed. On Restore the ring closes, each disputed province flashes gilt for about 1.5 s (keyframes "settle-flash") and takes its true colour, and a small parchment tag names who it went to. A deleted nation turns ruin grey with a dotted edge and its name is struck through until the neighbours have taken it.
