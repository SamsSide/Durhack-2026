the chrome-devtools MCP. Viewports: 1920x1080 (primary), 1920x800, 1440x900. One 6-nation world

## Checked and clean

- Create screen at all three viewports (fresh, a 900-character prompt, an image with a long file name, the
  saved worlds list): the framed sheet never scrolls, the body does; nothing leaves the frame.
- Prepare panel ("The world is ready", 30 of 30) and the READY 30/30 pill in the top bar; the top bar
  groups do not collide at 1440 px with the pill showing.
- Minimap, map-mode strip and the Terrain, Diplomacy, Wealth and Politics legends (keys 2, 3, 5, 6): no
  overlap with the minimap or the decree box at 1920x1080.
- HISTORY strip open (368 to 1512 px at 1920, 368 to 1032 px at 1440): clear of the decree box.
- Chronicle timeline open at 1440x900: covers the HISTORY strip, hides the minimap and decree, scrolls
  inside its own sheet.
- Nation card: all five tabs (Culture, Economy, Politics, History, Relations), no text leaving its box;
  the card scrolls inside its frame at 800 and 900 px tall.
- Network panel: graph with 7 nodes, a cut node, the picked-node tray, Treasury with 60 rows, Stats with
  `?mockStats=1`.
- Closing card (End key) at 1440x900.
- Console: no React warnings. The only errors are 503 and 404 responses from routes that are off in mock
  mode (voice).
