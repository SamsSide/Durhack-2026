## Post outline (fill in under an hour on Sunday)

Title: "[Project name]: [one-line what it does] in 24 hours at DurHack"
Tags: #mlhacks #devchallenge #hackathon (use the template link)

1. Hook (3 lines): the problem and why you cared.
2. What we built: one sentence, one screenshot or GIF, demo link and repo link.
3. How it works: architecture diagram, the 3 key components, a short code snippet of the clever part (10 to 20 lines).
4. The hardest bug or decision: what broke at 3am and how you fixed it.
5. How we used AI agents and sponsor tech (be honest and specific; name Vultr, etc.).
6. What we learned and what we would do next.
7. Credits and a question to readers to prompt comments.

Aim for 700 to 1200 words, 3 to 5 images, one code block, a working demo link.

## Maximising community popularity honestly

- Post early in the window so it has time to gather reactions.
- Strong title and cover image. Add a short real demo GIF.
- Share the link with the team, DurHack and MLH Discords, and friends who will genuinely read it. Do not ask for blind likes or use vote rings.
- Reply to every comment.
- Cross-link from the Devpost entry and the README.
