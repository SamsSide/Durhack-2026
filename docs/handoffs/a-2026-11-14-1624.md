## Gotchas
- `pr.sh merge` sometimes fails with "Base branch was modified": retry after 5 s (loop 3 times).
- Each worktree needs its own `npm install` (a symlinked node_modules breaks workspaces), and `.claude/worktrees/` is git-ignored.
- Never `pkill -f` with a pattern that matches your own shell (exit 144); kill by PID or `fuser -k PORT/tcp`.
- After a rebase, the main checkout may need `npm install` before `./check.sh` (missing `@google/genai`, `pg`).
- Adding a value to a shared union (TechAge, BuildingStyle, ChronicleKind) breaks exhaustive `Record`s in other lanes: fill them in the same PR.
- `lanerow.py` helper lived in the old scratchpad; recreate it or edit `docs/lanes/a.md` by hand.
